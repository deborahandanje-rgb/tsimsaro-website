# Tsimsaro Investment Limited Website

Official website for Tsimsaro Investment Limited, Bungoma, Kenya.

**Tagline:** *Turning Waste into Wealth: Empowering Sustainable Food Production.*

## Website pages

- `index.html` — Home
- `products.html` — Fresh fruits, pumpkin, KUNDE BITES, SEVEVE BITES and organic manure
- `services.html` — Monthly waste collection and demonstration farm training
- `about.html` — Company story and circular economy approach
- `farm.html` — Farm activities and impact
- `contact.html` — Product orders and service enquiries
- `css/styles.css` — Responsive styles
- `js/script.js` — Mobile navigation, enquiry form and dynamic year

## Before publishing

1. Upload the original Tsimsaro logo as `images/tsimsaro-logo.png`.
2. Replace illustrative stock photography with approved photographs of Tsimsaro's farm, products, packaging and team.
3. Add the official business phone/WhatsApp number and email to `contact.html`.
4. In `js/script.js`, set `businessWhatsApp` to the WhatsApp number in international digits-only format (Kenya example: `2547XXXXXXXX`). Do not include a plus sign or spaces.
5. Test all product links, mobile navigation and the enquiry form.
6. Confirm product availability, prices, delivery arrangements and service coverage.

The contact form does not send messages to a server by itself. It opens WhatsApp only after a business number is configured. For email-based submissions, connect a form provider such as Formspree and configure it before launch.

## Product prices currently displayed

- KUNDE BITES: KSh 250 per 100 g pack
- SEVEVE BITES: KSh 250 per 100 g pack
- Pumpkin: KSh 120 per kg
- Fresh or composted goat/rabbit manure: KSh 2,500 per 50 kg sack
- Mangoes, avocadoes, jackfruit and bananas: price on enquiry

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Open the repository on GitHub.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
4. Select branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`, then save.
5. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish and open the URL displayed in the Pages settings.

## Technology

Semantic HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No build step is required.
