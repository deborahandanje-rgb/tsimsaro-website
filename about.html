Who we are

An agribusiness with a circular mindset.

Tsimsaro Investment Limited is a sustainable agribusiness based in Bungoma, Kenya. We bring together food production, organic waste management and practical agricultural learning to make better use of resources.

Through our circular economy approach, suitable organic materials and animal manure can be transformed into compost and natural farm inputs. These resources help support crop production while keeping valuable nutrients in use.

Our work spans fresh farm produce, value-added dried African leafy vegetables, organic fertilizers, waste collection subscriptions and training through a demonstration farm.
